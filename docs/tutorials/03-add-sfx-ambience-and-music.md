# Tutorial 03 - Add SFX, Ambience, And Music

## Viewer Goal

The viewer adds Script Canvas markers for sound effects, ambience, and music, then understands that these become separate timeline tracks.

Target length: 4 to 5 minutes.

## Screen Recording Shot List

1. Open Script Canvas with a short two-speaker scene.
2. Add one `SFX` marker inside a line.
3. Add one `AMBIENCE` marker near the start of the scene.
4. Add one `MUSIC` marker for a short score bed.
5. Place the scene or full episode timeline.
6. Generate dialogue first.
7. Generate or batch-generate sound cues.
8. Show the timeline tracks overlapping dialogue.
9. Preview the mix.

## Demo Script To Type Or Import

```text
Mara: Rain filled the alley. [[AMBIENCE: heavy rain on city streets, distant traffic, no voices | scene]] [[MUSIC: low tense synth pulse, no vocals | 8s]]
Jonas: Keep moving.
Mara: Did you hear that? [[SFX: distant metal door slam, long warehouse echo | 1.5s]]
Jonas: I heard it. I just do not want it to hear us.
```

## Key Points To Say

- SFX is for short events.
- Ambience is for scene beds and room tone.
- Music is for score beds and stings.
- Markers stay inside a real spoken line.
- The app strips markers before TTS, then places audio cues on separate tracks.

## Recording Checklist

- Use `AMBIENCE`, not `BACKGROUND`, in the public tutorial.
- Keep prompts concrete and audio-focused.
- Show that cue clips are separate from dialogue clips.
- If the local sound sidecar is slow, show progress rather than cutting away too early.
- Mention that SFX and music are experimental and license-dependent.
